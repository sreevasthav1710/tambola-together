# GameHub User Manual

GameHub is an online game room app for friends. One player creates a room, shares the room code, and acts as the host. Every player gets an individual ticket for Tambola/Housie, marks called numbers, and claims prizes when their ticket matches a winning pattern.

## What You Need

- A room code from the host, or access to create a new room.
- A display name.
- A stable internet connection so called numbers, marks, claims, and player scores stay in sync.

## Game Basics

Each player receives one Tambola ticket.

- The ticket has 3 rows and 9 columns.
- Each row contains 5 numbers and 4 blank spaces.
- Each ticket has 15 numbers in total.
- Numbers are arranged by column:
  - Column 1: 1-9
  - Columns 2-8: 10-19, 20-29, and so on through 70-79
  - Column 9: 80-90

The host calls numbers from 1 to 90. A number can only be called once.

## Creating a Room

1. Open GameHub.
2. Select **Create Room**.
3. Enter your display name.
4. Set the prize amounts for:
   - Fastest Five
   - Top Line
   - Middle Line
   - Bottom Line
   - Housie
5. Set how many Housie winners are allowed.
6. Select **Create Room**.
7. Share the room code with the other players.

The room creator is also a player and receives a ticket.

## Joining a Room

1. Open GameHub.
2. Select **Join Room**.
3. Enter the 6-character room code shared by the host.
4. Enter your display name.
5. Select **Join**.

You will receive your own ticket after joining. If the room has already ended, you cannot join it.

## Host Controls

The host is responsible for calling numbers.

- Select **Next Number** to call a random number.
- The first called number changes the room from waiting to playing.
- The latest number appears as the last called number.
- The called count shows progress out of 90.
- The called numbers board highlights every number that has arrived.

Only the host can call the next number.

## Marking Your Ticket

When a number is called, find it on your ticket and select it to mark it.

- You can only mark numbers that have already been called.
- If you select an uncalled number, the app will reject it.
- Select a marked number again to unmark it.
- Keep your ticket accurate before claiming a prize.

## Prize Claims

Use the **Claim a prize** buttons when your ticket qualifies.

| Claim | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Fastest Five | Any 5 numbers on your ticket are called and marked. |
| Top Line | All 5 numbers in the top row are called and marked. |
| Middle Line | All 5 numbers in the middle row are called and marked. |
| Bottom Line | All 5 numbers in the bottom row are called and marked. |
| Housie | All 15 numbers on your ticket are called and marked. |

Fastest Five and each line prize can be claimed once per room. Housie can be claimed by the number of winners chosen when the room was created.

## Bogey Claims

A bogey is an incorrect claim.

If you claim too early or your required numbers are not fully marked, the app deducts that prize amount from your purse.

Examples:

- Claiming Top Line before all top-row numbers are called is a bogey.
- Claiming Housie before all 15 ticket numbers are marked is a bogey.
- Marking an uncalled number is not allowed.

## Purse and Scores

Each player has a purse.

- Correct claims add the prize amount to your purse.
- Bogey claims subtract the prize amount from your purse.
- The players list shows current purse values.
- The prizes claimed panel shows who has already won each prize.

## Ending the Game

The game ends automatically when the allowed number of Housie claims has been reached.

After the game ends:

- No more numbers are called.
- The leaderboard is shown.
- Players are ranked by purse from highest to lowest.

## Leaving a Room

Select **Exit** to leave the room.

Leaving removes your player record and you lose access to that ticket. The app asks for confirmation before exiting.

## Tips for Players

- Watch the last called number and the called numbers board.
- Mark numbers immediately after they arrive.
- Check your whole row or ticket before claiming.
- Avoid guessing; a wrong claim reduces your purse.

## Tips for Hosts

- Share the room code clearly before starting.
- Wait until all expected players have joined.
- Call numbers at a steady pace.
- Remind players that Housie ends the game once the configured winner limit is reached.
